Genius Gym

A private, personal tool for uploading and scheduling Shorts to my own YouTube channels.

What it does

Genius Gym uploads short videos that I have made to YouTube channels that I own, and sets each video's title, description, tags, category and audience setting. Videos are uploaded as private and scheduled to go public at a time I choose. Before uploading, it reads the connected channel's name to confirm it is uploading to the right channel.

Who it is for

Only me. It is a personal tool operated by Aseem Bhalla for YouTube channels he owns. It is not a public service and does not accept other users. It can upload new videos to the connected channel; it does not delete videos.
