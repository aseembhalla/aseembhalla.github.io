Channel Monitor

A private, personal tool for reviewing my own YouTube channels' performance.

What it does

Channel Monitor reads public statistics and YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, retention, traffic sources and audience summaries) for YouTube channels that I own, and saves them so I can track how my videos perform over time.

Who it is for

Only me. It is not a public service and does not accept other users. Access is read-only: it cannot upload, edit or delete anything on YouTube.
