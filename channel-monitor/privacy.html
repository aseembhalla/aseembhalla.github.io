Privacy Policy

Channel Monitor · Last updated 24 November 2026

Channel Monitor is a personal tool operated by Aseem Bhalla to review the performance of YouTube channels he owns. This policy explains what data it accesses and how that data is handled.

Data accessed

When a channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission, Channel Monitor requests read-only access through these scopes:

  • youtube.readonly: to identify which channel was connected and list its videos.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: to read YouTube Analytics reports for that channel (for example views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources, and aggregated, anonymous audience demographics).

It also reads public video and channel statistics through the YouTube Data API. It does not access email, contacts, Drive files or any other Google data, and it cannot upload, modify or delete any content.

How data is used

The data is used only to produce performance reports for the channel owner's own channels. Reports are stored in a private repository accessible only to the owner, and may be analysed by tools the owner chooses to use, including AI assistants, solely to provide those reports.

Sharing

Data is not sold, rented, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. It is not used to train generalised AI or machine-learning models.

Storage and retention

Access credentials are stored as encrypted secrets and are never published. Report data is kept in the owner's private repository until the owner deletes it.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or through the Google security settings page. After revocation, no further data can be read.

Google API Services

Channel Monitor's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Channel Monitor uses YouTube API Services; by using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
